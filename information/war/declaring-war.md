# Declaring War

To declare war, click on the country's land, Click Profile > Relations > Justify War > Select Reasons for War, then you must wait out for about 120 seconds, after the goal is justified, you do the same process you did earlier but instead, the Justify War Button will be replaced with "Declare War". Once you have clicked the button, the war has begun.

#### Reasons to declare war

| Prompt     |
| ---------- |
| Liberation |
| Conquer    |
| Coup       |

#### Will the enemy state know I justified a war goal?

Yes, they will get a notification, when that's the case, and you are the enemy state, you must prepare your military to protect your nation.

#### Can I cancel the justification?

Yes you can, but it might still alert the enemy state.
