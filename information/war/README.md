# War

When a country declares war against you, you are to fully protect your nation at all cost to prevent occupation. This means you must recruit a military, when done, send your military to the nation that declared war on you to fight, do not send all, keep some in your country in case the person sends military to your country, rememeber not to get occupied.

During war, there will be a progress bar, when its filled, it means you have occupied that city/region, when you occupy the entire country, there will be a proposal to end the war with conditions that both parties can select, either you can take over the entire country under your control, take patrial land, or withdraw completely, it is up to you.

In some cases, once a capital gets occupied, the capital is moved elsewhere, so make sure to occupy other cities to prevent that.

Some nations might have multiple capitals, it is recommended you occupy the non capitals and then split the military to occupy the capitals at the same time.
