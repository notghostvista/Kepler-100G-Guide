# Sanctions and Embargoes

Sanctions and/or Embargoes are a set of restrictions that one nation can impose on another country if it is believed that said country has been commiting crimes. Both Sanctions and Embargoes have their differences and are used for different situations

#### Sanctions:

Sanctions are meant to pressure a government to change their ways, which is a more lenient than an embargo, the affects of sanctions are:

* Freezing Assets
* Travel Ban on a specific person
* Block on trade of certain products
* Limited access to tech and banking

#### Embargoes

Embargoes are the harsher punishments that a country can impose, its basically a full cut off of certain goods, the affects of embargoes are:<br>

* Full Trade Ban (can be targeted on certain goods)
* Full Travel Ban on certain figures
* Ban of use of technology from said country

Such action can affect a nation, it is recommended to keep your nation stable to avoid possible sanctions/embargoes
