# Sanction/Embargo Imposed Against You

A country can impose a sanction/embargo against you if they believe it is for the same reasons you can impose a sanction against another country

#### Reasons on why a country can Impose Sanctions/Embargoes agaisnt you

* Espionage
* Terrorism
* War
* Human Rights Violations
* Violation of International Law
* Oppresive Regime

To know you have been sanctioned/embargoed, you will get a popup that will say the following\
"Alert"\
"\[Country] has imposed a \[type of sanction/embargo] against you"\
Prompt to close: What a pathetic nation

If the "Social Republic of Skibidia" imposes a sanction against you the popup will say\
"Alert"\
"You have been sanctioned by Skibidia, you can't sanction us back and you cannot travel to us at all".\
In Singleplayer mode, this popup will show up, in Online Multiplayer, it'll be the normal popup as any other country\
\
Note that Skibidia can sanction you at any time for any given reason, however their sanctions will not affect you at all if you are playing singleplayer mode, to avoid this, don't inteact with them at all. If you are playing as Skibidia, you can do the same but your sanctions will not affect anyone, unless you are playing Online Multiplayer.
