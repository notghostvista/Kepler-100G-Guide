# Impose a Sanction/Embargo

To impose a sanction, click on the country you want to sanction, Click on the country either from land or the country list, Click Profile > Economics > Restrictions > Impose a Sanction (or Impose an Embargo), and click on one of the reasons on why you will impose restrictions.

To prevent abuse of the sanction system, a nation can sanction one country per 30 minutes, and the nation must be unstable and known to have broken international law.

Sanctions/Embargoes can last up to 1 in game year (about 146 hours).&#x20;

#### Reasons to Impose Sanctions/Embargoes

* Espionage
* Terrorism
* War
* Human Rights Violations
* Violation of International Law
* Oppresive Regime

When you want to impose a restriction, the country must meet one of those requirements, not because its based off your opinion, it'll happen at its own pace and randomly.<br>

A country may also impose a sanction/embargo against you.
