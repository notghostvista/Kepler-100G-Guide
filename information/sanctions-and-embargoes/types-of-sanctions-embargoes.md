# Types of Sanctions/Embargoes

There are multiple types of Sanctions/Embargoes, you can be specific on which type of restrictions you would want to impose. Economically, sanctions/embargoes might not affect certain countries, it all depends on how weathly they are.

| Type                      | Affect                                                    | Outcomes                                                                        |
| ------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Tariff                    | Impose a tax on imported items                            | Inflated prices depending on how strong the tariff is                           |
| Trade Block               | Blocks Imported Items                                     | Prices inflate highly on item                                                   |
| Tech Ban                  | Bans use of technology that was created from said country | Civilians can not use any tech from the country that sanctioned                 |
| Travel Ban                | Bans all travel from a country                            | Less tourism, affects economics of your country                                 |
| Embargo on Specific Goods | Severe Restrictions on certain goods                      | Ban of all purchases, trades, and use of certain goods from sanctioned country. |
