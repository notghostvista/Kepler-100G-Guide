# Manage Your Nation

Managing your nation comes with a lot of responsibilites, this includes protecting your nation and managing the stability of your nation.

In order to keep your nation stable, you must have a decent military presence, and very good border security, this is to keep any bad guys from entering your nation to take over. Another way is to keep your constitution fair and stable, this is so no seperatist movements or unrest are invoked, which can cause economic issues and reputable harm.
